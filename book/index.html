By Zoltan L. Deak

STOP!… And Read This Before Making Your First Film

A Real-World Guide to Making — and Selling — Your First Feature Film

Second Edition — Revised and Expanded

Survive the business

Most filmmaking books tell you how to hold a camera. This one tells you how to survive the business. Drawing on decades of real-world production experience, Zoltan L. Deak delivers the brutally honest, practical guidance that film schools don’t teach — from development through distribution and everything in between.

Get the book

What’s inside

Not another theoretical filmmaking manual — a battle-tested roadmap from someone who’s been in the trenches, from the spark of an idea to getting your film in front of audiences.

  • Development & Financing

    How to develop a viable project and navigate the complex world of film financing without losing your shirt — or your mind.

  • Pre-Production Realities

    The critical decisions that make or break your production before cameras ever roll. Budgeting, scheduling, and assembling your team.

  • Production Survival

    What actually happens on set and how to handle the chaos, compromises, and creative challenges of shooting your first feature.

  • Post-Production

    Editing, sound, music, VFX, and deliverables — the unglamorous but essential phase that turns footage into a finished film.

  • Distribution & Sales

    The part nobody tells you about. How to actually sell your film, navigate distributors, and understand the marketplace.

  • The Business of Film

    Contracts, deals, markets, and the industry realities that determine whether your film — and your career — has a future.

Who this book is for

  • First-time filmmakers ready to move from dreaming to doing
  • Independent producers who want to avoid costly mistakes
  • Film students who want the education they’re not getting in school
  • Anyone who’s ever thought about making a feature film but didn’t know where to start
  • Experienced creators looking to understand the business side

About the author

Zoltan L. Deak is a producer and filmmaker with decades of experience in international film production. As the founder of Pillango Productions, he brings a perspective that bridges European and Hollywood filmmaking traditions — combining creative vision with hard-won business acumen.

This second edition has been revised and expanded to reflect the rapidly evolving landscape of film production and distribution, including the impact of streaming platforms, AI-assisted workflows and new financing models.
