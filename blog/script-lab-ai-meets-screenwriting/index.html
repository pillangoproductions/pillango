Script Lab: AI Meets Screenwriting

Our journey building an intelligent script analysis tool that helps writers refine their craft.

I have read a large amount of screenplays. Some were brilliant. Some were not ready. Most were somewhere in between — scripts with genuine potential that needed honest, detailed feedback to get where they needed to go.

And that is exactly where the problem lies. Getting quality feedback on a screenplay is harder than it should be.

Professional script coverage is one of the most valuable tools a writer can invest in. I have written about this before, and I stand by it. But I also know the reality. Good coverage is not always accessible. It can be expensive, turnaround times vary wildly, and the quality depends entirely on who is reading your script. Some readers are exceptional. Others give you a page of vague notes that tell you nothing you did not already know.

I kept thinking there had to be a better way. Not a replacement for human insight — I want to be very clear about that — but a tool that could give writers immediate, structured, intelligent feedback on their scripts. Something that analyses the fundamentals with consistency and depth. Something available at any hour, at a fraction of the cost, and ready the moment the writer is.

That is why we built Pillango Script Lab.

Why We Built It

This did not start as a technology project. It started as a producing problem.

When you are evaluating scripts for potential development, you need to assess them quickly and thoroughly. Is the structure sound? Does the protagonist have a clear arc? Is the concept marketable? Does the dialogue serve the story or slow it down? These are the questions every producer asks, and answering them properly takes time — time that multiplies with every script that crosses your desk.

I wanted a tool that could handle the first pass. Not to replace the creative judgment that comes with experience, but to do the analytical heavy lifting — the structural breakdown, the character mapping, the pacing analysis — so that human readers could focus on the questions that actually require a human mind.

The more we developed it, the more I realised this was not just useful for producers. It was potentially transformative for writers.

What Script Lab Actually Does

Pillango Script Lab is an AI-powered script coverage tool. You upload your screenplay, and it generates a detailed analysis covering the areas that matter most: story structure, character development, dialogue, pacing, theme, and marketability.

It does not give you a generic summary and a thumbs up. It gives you a proper coverage report — the kind of breakdown a professional reader would provide, with specific observations about what is working, what is not, and where the opportunities for improvement are.

Think of it as having a well-read, analytically rigorous development executive available to you at any time. One who has been trained on the principles that separate scripts that move forward from scripts that sit in a drawer.

Is it perfect? No. No tool is, and I would not trust one that claimed to be. But it is consistent, it is thorough, and it catches things that even experienced readers sometimes miss — particularly in areas like structural pacing and character arc tracking, where the analysis benefits from the kind of systematic attention that AI handles exceptionally well.

The Question Everyone Asks

"Can AI really understand a screenplay?"

It is a fair question, and I asked it myself before we started building this. The honest answer is nuanced.

Can AI understand the way a human reader does — feeling the rhythm of a scene, sensing when a moment of dialogue rings true, recognising the indefinable quality that makes a script special? No. Not yet, and perhaps not ever. That kind of understanding comes from lived experience, and I do not believe any tool can fully replicate it.

But can AI identify structural weaknesses, track character arcs across 120 pages, assess dialogue patterns, evaluate pacing, and flag the kinds of problems that derail scripts in development? Absolutely. And it can do it with a level of consistency and speed that is simply not possible for a human reader processing dozens of scripts.

The key is understanding what the tool is and what it is not. Script Lab is not your creative partner. It is your analytical one. It does the rigorous, systematic work so that you can focus on the creative decisions that only you can make.

What This Means for Writers

If you are a screenwriter, here is what Script Lab gives you that is hard to get anywhere else.

First, immediacy. You finish a draft at two in the morning, you want feedback, and you do not want to wait two weeks for it. Script Lab is there when you are.

Second, objectivity. The tool has no relationship with you. It has no stake in your project. It does not care about your feelings. It analyses what is on the page, nothing more and nothing less. That kind of clean, unbiased assessment is exactly what writers need during the rewriting process.

Third, affordability. Professional coverage from a reputable service can cost hundreds of dollars per read. If you are on your third or fourth draft and you want fresh eyes each time, those costs add up quickly. Script Lab makes it possible to get detailed feedback at every stage of your writing process without breaking the bank.

And fourth, consistency. Every analysis is built on the same foundational principles. You are not at the mercy of whether your reader had a good day or whether they happen to connect with your genre. The assessment is structured, repeatable, and reliable.

How I Use It as a Producer

I still read every script myself. That has not changed and it will not. But Script Lab has fundamentally changed how I work through my reading pile.

When a script comes in, I run it through Script Lab first. The coverage report gives me a clear picture of the structural and technical foundations before I sit down to read. That means when I do read, I am not spending mental energy on basic diagnostics. I already know where the potential issues are. I can focus instead on the things that matter most at the producing level — the voice, the vision, the commercial viability, the intangible quality that makes me want to pick up the phone and talk to the writer.

It has made me a more efficient reader and, I believe, a better one. It has not replaced my judgment. It has sharpened it.

The Bottom Line

We built Pillango Script Lab because we believe every screenplay deserves serious, thoughtful analysis — and that access to that analysis should not depend on your budget or your connections.

Whether you are a first-time writer looking for honest feedback on your debut feature, or a working screenwriter polishing a draft before sending it to your agent, Script Lab is designed to help you see your script more clearly and make it stronger.

It is not a shortcut. It is a tool. And like every good tool, it works best in the hands of someone who is serious about their craft.
