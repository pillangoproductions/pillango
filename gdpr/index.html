Legal

GDPR

Your data rights, and how to use them.

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the following rights over the personal data Pillango Productions Kft. holds about you. What we process, and why, is described in our privacy notice.

Your rights

  • Access (Art. 15) — ask whether we hold data about you and receive a copy.
  • Rectification (Art. 16) — have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Erasure (Art. 17) — have your data deleted where there is no reason to keep it.
  • Restriction (Art. 18) — ask us to pause processing while a question is resolved.
  • Portability (Art. 20) — receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection (Art. 21) — object to processing based on our legitimate interest.

How to make a request

E-mail hello@pillangoprod.com with the subject “GDPR request”. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We respond within one month, free of charge.

Complaints

If you think we have handled your data unlawfully, please contact us first — we will try to put it right. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hungarian supervisory authority:

Authority
Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH)
Address
1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11.
Web
naih.hu

You may also go to court; the case can be brought before the regional court (törvényszék) of your place of residence.
