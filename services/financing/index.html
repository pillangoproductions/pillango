Post-Production Financing

Flexible financing solutions for filmmakers, in partnership with M2 Media Post.

Finishing a film costs money at exactly the moment most budgets run dry. Together with M2 Media Post of Beverly Hills, we offer filmmakers flexible financing for post-production — so the edit, the sound and the delivery don’t have to wait for the next tranche, the sales advance or the incentive payment.

Who it is for

  • Feature films, documentaries and TV series in or approaching post-production
  • Productions waiting on a rebate or tax incentive that arrives after the spend
  • Producers in Europe, the USA and Canada

How it works

Every project is different. Send us where you are — budget, schedule, what’s already secured and what’s still to come — and we’ll look at a structure that fits, together with M2 Media Post. Post-production can then run at Pécs City Studios or with your own team.
