Production Consulting

Production strategy for European projects, including Hungary’s competitive film incentives.

Producers coming to Hungary for the first time often spend weeks getting conflicting answers about how the incentive actually works against their budget. We give you a straight picture, based on current practice.

What we cover

  • How the 30% rebate (effectively up to 37.5%) works against your actual budget
  • What it covers, what it doesn’t, and where producers get caught out
  • The current state of the system — registration timing, caps and payment cycles
  • Structuring, co-production and partners in Hungary and across Europe
  • Whether Hungary genuinely makes sense for your project — or whether you’d be better off elsewhere

Location finding

For incoming productions we find, photograph and clear locations across Pécs and Southern Hungary — and further afield. We know the region’s mines and industrial sites, castles and palaces, lakes, forests, villages and modern architecture, and we handle the owners, permits and logistics so your scout arrives to a shortlist, not a guess.

  • Location research and scouting to your script and look book
  • Photo packages and recces with your director and designer
  • Owner contact, permits, fees and scheduling
