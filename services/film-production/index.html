Shooting in Hungary

Hungary offers one of Europe’s most competitive film incentives — a 30% cash rebate on qualifying spend — together with experienced crews, studios and locations that double for much of the world. We help productions from Europe, the USA and Canada structure their shoot around it: budget, schedule, partners and paperwork.
