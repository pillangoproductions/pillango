Cookie policy

No advertising cookies. Statistics and video only if you agree.

This policy explains which cookies and similar browser technologies pillangoprod.com, operated by Pillango Productions Kft., uses and why. It follows the EU ePrivacy rules as applied in Hungary (Act C of 2003 on Electronic Communications, § 155(4)) and the GDPR. How we handle personal data in general is described in our privacy notice.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and session storage, keep small pieces of information in your browser in the same way. The law treats them alike, so this policy covers all of them.

Your choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies and YouTube previews. Both buttons are equal: “Accept all” or “Necessary only”. Nothing optional is set until you choose “Accept all”, and you can change your mind at any time:

What we do not use

  • No advertising, marketing or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No social media plug-ins.
  • No third-party web fonts — typefaces are served from our own host.

Strictly necessary (always on)

These are needed for features you ask for, contain no personal data, and never leave your browser except where stated. Under the law they do not require consent.

Your cookie choice (local storage)

Name
pillango-consent
Purpose
Remembers whether you chose “Accept all” or “Necessary only”, so the banner does not ask again.
Duration
Until you change your choice or clear your browser data

Private project sign-in (cookie)

Name
pillango_hs
Type
First-party session cookie, strictly necessary
Purpose
Keeps you signed in after you enter the password for a private project page. Set only if you enter a password; only sent to the private project pages.
Duration
Deleted when you close the browser or sign out

Page transitions (session storage)

Names
pillango-transit, pillango-arrive
Type
Session storage, strictly necessary for the site’s navigation
Purpose
Remembers, for a moment, which page you came from and the direction of travel, so the move to the next page is smooth.
Duration
Read and removed as soon as the next page opens; in any case deleted when you close the tab

Location favourites (local storage)

Name
pillango-location-favourites
Type
Local storage, set only when you use the feature
Purpose
Keeps the list of locations you mark as favourites on the location database page. It reaches us only if you choose to send it.
Duration
Until you remove the favourites or clear your browser data

Only with your consent

Visitor statistics — Google Analytics

Names
_ga, _ga_<ID>
Provider
Google Ireland Ltd. (first-party cookies on pillangoprod.com)
Purpose
Counts visits and shows us, in aggregate, how the site is used. Not used for advertising.
Duration
Up to 2 years; deleted straight away if you switch to “Necessary only”

Videos — YouTube

Provider
Google Ireland Ltd. (YouTube, privacy-enhanced mode: youtube-nocookie.com)
When
Only on the work page, and only after you accept cookies (preview images) or press play (the video player)
Purpose
Plays the video. YouTube may store cookies or local storage in your browser for playback, settings and statistics, as described in Google’s cookie policy.
Duration
Set by Google — from the end of the session up to several months

Browser settings

You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. The site works without them; you will only need to enter a private project password again, your favourites list will be empty, page changes will start at the top, and the cookie banner will ask again.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to partner sites and other websites. Once you follow such a link, that site’s own cookie policy applies.

Changes

If we add or change cookies, we will update this policy first and, where consent is needed, ask for it again.
