Cookie policy

No tracking, no advertising, no banner needed.

This policy explains which cookies and similar browser technologies pillangoprod.com, operated by Pillango Productions Kft., uses and why. It follows the EU ePrivacy rules as applied in Hungary (Act C of 2003 on Electronic Communications, § 155(4)) and the GDPR. How we handle personal data in general is described in our privacy notice.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and session storage, keep small pieces of information in your browser in the same way. The law treats them alike, so this policy covers all of them.

What we do not use

  • No analytics or statistics cookies.
  • No advertising, marketing or tracking cookies.
  • No social media plug-ins, embedded videos or other third-party content that could set its own cookies.
  • No third-party scripts or web fonts — everything is served from our own host.

What we use

Everything below is strictly necessary for a feature you ask for, contains no personal data, and never leaves your browser except where stated. Under the law, strictly necessary cookies do not require consent, which is why the site shows no cookie banner.

Private project sign-in (cookie)

Name
pillango_hs
Type
First-party session cookie, strictly necessary
Purpose
Keeps you signed in after you enter the password for a private project page. Set only if you enter a password; only sent to the private project pages.
Duration
Deleted when you close the browser or sign out

Page transitions (session storage)

Names
pillango-transit, pillango-arrive
Type
Session storage, strictly necessary for the site’s navigation
Purpose
Remembers, for a moment, which page you came from and the direction of travel, so the move to the next page is smooth.
Duration
Read and removed as soon as the next page opens; in any case deleted when you close the tab

Location favourites (local storage)

Name
pillango-location-favourites
Type
Local storage, set only when you use the feature
Purpose
Keeps the list of locations you mark as favourites on the location database page. It reaches us only if you choose to send it.
Duration
Until you remove the favourites or clear your browser data

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings. The site works without them; you will only need to enter a private project password again, your favourites list will be empty, and page changes will start at the top.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to partner sites and other websites. Once you follow such a link, that site’s own cookie policy applies.

Changes

If we ever add cookies that need your consent — for example analytics — we will update this policy first and ask for your consent before setting them.
