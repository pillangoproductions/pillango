Privacy notice

No tracking, and we only use what you send us to reply.

This notice explains what personal data Pillango Productions Kft. (“we”) processes when you visit pillangoprod.com or contact us, why, and for how long. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Hungarian Act CXII of 2011 (Infotv.). Your rights under the GDPR are summarised on the GDPR page.

Data controller

Pillango Productions Kft., 7632 Pécs, Közraktár utca 5, Hungary, company reg. no. Cg. 02-09-088532. Contact: contact form.

What this website does not do

  • It uses no advertising or marketing tools.
  • It loads nothing from Google or YouTube unless you agree — fonts and everything else are served from our own host.
  • It has no accounts or newsletter sign-up; the only form is the contact form.

Cookies

Without your consent the site uses only strictly necessary cookies and storage — for example, a session cookie that keeps you signed in to a private project page, and a note of your cookie choice. Analytics cookies and YouTube previews are used only if you press “Accept all” in the cookie banner. Full details, and a button to change your choice, are in our cookie policy.

Visitor statistics (Google Analytics)

If you accept, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used — which pages are visited, roughly from where, on what kind of device, and how visitors arrive. We use this only in aggregate to improve the site; we do not use it for advertising and do not try to identify you. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses for EU visitors.

Legal basis
Your consent — GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); you can withdraw it at any time on the cookie policy page
Processor
Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland
Retention
Up to 14 months, after which Google deletes the data

Videos (YouTube)

The Our work and Films we represent pages show videos hosted on YouTube, embedded in privacy-enhanced mode. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play or accept cookies. When a video loads, Google Ireland Ltd. receives your IP address and technical browser data and may store cookies or similar data in your browser, under Google’s privacy policy.

Legal basis
Your consent — GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), given by accepting cookies or pressing play

Location database

If you save favourites on our location database page, the list is kept only in your own browser (local storage) and reaches us only if you choose to send it.

Session storage

To make the transition between pages smooth, the site keeps a small technical note in your browser’s session storage (which page you came from and the direction of travel). It contains no personal data, is never sent to us and is deleted when you close the tab.

Server logs

Like every web server, our host records technical request data (IP address, date and time, requested page, browser user-agent, referrer) to keep the site secure and working.

Purpose
Security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting
Legal basis
Legitimate interest — GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)
Processor
DreamHost, LLC (USA) — hosting provider
Retention
For a limited period under the host’s standard log rotation, then deleted

When you contact us

If you send us a message through the contact form, we process your name, e-mail address, the topic you chose and whatever you include in your message, in order to reply and — if we work together — to prepare and carry out the project. The form is delivered to our mailbox by e-mail; nothing is stored on the website.

Legal basis
Steps prior to entering a contract — GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); otherwise legitimate interest — Art. 6(1)(f)
Retention
Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 2 years. Project correspondence: as long as required by Hungarian accounting and tax law.
Processors
DreamHost, LLC (sends the form by e-mail); our e-mail provider

International transfers

Our hosting provider is based in the United States, and Google may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.
