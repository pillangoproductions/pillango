About

An independent studio, built around the film.

Pillango Productions Kft. is a Hungarian production company working in production, post-production and production consulting — for our own projects and for partners across Europe.

Scroll

01:00:03:00The name

Pillangó — butterfly.

Small, patient, and changed completely by the process. It is how we think about films: the work happens quietly, in stages, and what comes out the other end should look like it could only ever have been that.

01:00:09:16Approach

One team from first page to final file.

We keep the company small on purpose. The people who read your script are the people who plan the shoot, sit in the edit and sign off the deliverables.

  • DevelopStory & planScript, budget, financing
  • ShootProductionCrew, locations, schedule
  • FinishPostEdit, colour, sound, delivery

01:00:15:02Where we work

Rooted in Hungary, open to Europe.

Hungary has deep crews, varied locations and a well-established film incentive. We help productions use all three — as producer, co-producer or local service partner — and we bring European partners into our own projects.
