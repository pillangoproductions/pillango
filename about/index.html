About Pillango Productions

Pillango Productions is a Hungary-based film production company operating at the intersection of storytelling, technology, and global collaboration.

What we make

We produce feature films, vertical series, branded content, and high-impact advertising — combining traditional production expertise with next-generation AI-driven workflows. By integrating AI-assisted development, pre-visualization, and scalable content pipelines, we reduce production costs while increasing creative flexibility.

For today’s platforms

From cinematic feature films to fast-moving vertical dramas and digital campaigns, we create content designed for today’s platforms — and tomorrow’s audiences.
