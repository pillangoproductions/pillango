About Pillango Productions

Pillango Productions is a Hungary-based film production company operating at the intersection of storytelling, technology, and global collaboration.

What we make

We develop and produce feature films and TV series, alongside branded content and high-impact advertising. For series, we work from the first concept through development, packaging and financing to production and delivery — combining traditional production expertise with next-generation AI-driven workflows that reduce costs while increasing creative flexibility.

Development is where a series is won: we shape the concept, the pilot and the series bible, build the package and bring in the partners who can take it to the screen.

For today’s platforms

From cinematic feature films to returning TV series and brand campaigns, we make work for broadcasters, streamers and cinemas — built for today’s platforms and tomorrow’s audiences.

  • Feature films

    Cinematic features and documentaries.

  • TV series

    Series development and production, from concept to delivery.

  • Brands & ads

    Branded content and advertising.

Hungary, Europe & North America

With partners in Hungary, across Europe, in the USA and in Canada, Pillango Productions brings creative leadership and smart production strategy to both sides of the Atlantic. We know the European production ecosystems — including Hungary’s competitive film incentives — and help North American and European producers find the right place, partners and structure for their projects.
